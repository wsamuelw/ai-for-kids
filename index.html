⬜ Step 1 of 6

What is AI?

AI stands for Artificial Intelligence. It's like teaching a computer to learn — just like you!

🤖
Bolt says:
Think of AI like teaching your pet dog new tricks. You show the dog what to do, and after lots of practice, it learns! AI works the same way — but instead of a dog, it's a computer!
📚

Learns from Examples

Show a computer thousands of cat photos, and it learns what a cat looks like — just like you learned your ABCs!

🧩

Solves Problems

AI can figure out puzzles and find patterns — sometimes faster than grown-ups!

🎯

Gets Better with Practice

The more AI practises, the smarter it gets — just like riding a bike!

Quick Check! 🤔

How does AI learn?

Parent tip: Ask your child: "Can you think of something YOU learned by looking at lots of examples?"

Staying Safe with AI

AI is awesome, but we need to be smart about using it!

🤖
Bolt says:
I'm smart, but I make mistakes sometimes! Always remember: AI is a helper, not a replacement for grown-ups. Here are some important rules!
🤔

AI Makes Mistakes

AI can be wrong sometimes. Always double-check important answers with a grown-up!

🔒

Keep Secrets Safe

Never share your name, address, school, or photos with AI. Talk to a grown-up first!

👨‍👩‍👧

Ask a Grown-Up

Before using any AI tool, always ask a parent or teacher to help you. It's more fun together anyway!

Quick Check! 🤔

What should you do before using an AI tool?

Parent tip: Discuss digital citizenship together. Explain that AI is a tool like a hammer — useful when used correctly, but you need to be careful.
